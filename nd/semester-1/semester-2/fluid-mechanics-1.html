MEC 122: Fluid Mechanics I

Module 1: Fluid Properties, Hydrostatic Pressure, and Flow Continuity

1.1 Fundamental Properties of Fluids

A fluid is a substance that deforms continuously under the application of shear stress, no matter how small. Fluids encompass both liquids and gases.

Key Physical Properties

  • Mass Density ($\rho$): Mass per unit volume ($\text{kg/m}^3$). For pure water at $4^\circ\text{C}$, $\rho = 1000 \, \text{kg/m}^3$.
  • Specific Weight ($\gamma$): Weight force exerted per unit volume ($\text{N/m}^3$), defined as $\gamma = \rho g$.
  • Dynamic Viscosity ($\mu$): Internal fluid resistance to flow shear ($\text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$ or $\text{N}\cdot\text{s/m}^2$).
  • Kinematic Viscosity ($\nu$): Ratio of dynamic viscosity to density ($\text{m}^2/\text{s}$), defined as $\nu = \frac{\mu}{\rho}$.
Newton's Law of Viscosity

$$\tau = \mu \frac{du}{dy}$$

Where $\tau$ is shear stress ($\text{N/m}^2$) and $\frac{du}{dy}$ is velocity gradient perpendicular to flow direction.

1.2 Hydrostatic Pressure & Pascal's Law

Hydrostatics studies fluid equilibrium at rest. Pressure exerted at any point in a static fluid acts equally in all directions.

Hydrostatic Equation

$$P = P_0 + \rho g h$$

Where $P_0$ is surface atmospheric pressure, $\rho$ is fluid density, $g$ is gravitational acceleration ($9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2$), and $h$ is fluid column depth ($\text{m}$).

Continuity Equation for Incompressible Flow

$$Q = A_1 V_1 = A_2 V_2 = \text{Constant}$$
