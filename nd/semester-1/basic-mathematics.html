MTH 111: Basic Mathematics I

Module 1: Algebra, Partial Fractions, Trigonometry, and Coordinate Geometry

1.1 Quadratic Equations & Polynomial Roots

Algebraic formulation underpins structural and thermodynamic equations across mechanical engineering. A quadratic equation in standard polynomial form is expressed as $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.

Quadratic Formula & Discriminant ($\Delta$)

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

The discriminant $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$ dictates root characteristics: real and distinct ($\Delta > 0$), equal ($\Delta = 0$), or complex conjugate ($\Delta < 0$).

1.2 Partial Fraction Decomposition

Partial fraction resolution simplifies complex rational expressions into integrable components—an essential technique for dynamic system analysis and Laplace transforms.

Distinct Linear Factors

$$\frac{P(x)}{(x - a)(x - b)} = \frac{A}{x - a} + \frac{B}{x - b}$$

1.3 Trigonometric Identities & Vector Triangle Rules

Trigonometric rules resolve force vectors, linkage angles, and harmonic wave motions in mechanical design.

Sine & Cosine Laws for Oblique Triangles

$$\text{Sine Law:} \quad \frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$$

$$\text{Cosine Law:} \quad c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos(C)$$
