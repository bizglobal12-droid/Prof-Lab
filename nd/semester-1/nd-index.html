National Diploma (ND) Engineering Curriculum

Complete semester breakdown of mechanical engineering courses and lecture modules

ND Year 1 — Semester 1

Foundational mechanics, engineering drawing conventions, workshop practice, material science, and pure mathematics.

Semester 1 Modules
  • MEC 111: Mechanical Engineering Science I

    Statics, forces, moments, kinematics, work-energy, and friction.

  • MEC 112: Engineering Drawing I

    ISO line standards, geometric constructions, orthographic, and pictorial projections.

  • MEC 113: Workshop Technology & Practice I

    Workshop safety codes, hand tools, bench fitting, and sheet metal fabrication.

  • MEC 114: Engineering Materials I

    Material classification, mechanical properties, ferrous, and non-ferrous alloys.

  • MTH 111: Basic Mathematics I

    Algebraic identities, partial fractions, trigonometry, and coordinate geometry.

ND Year 1 — Semester 2

Strength of materials, fluid mechanics, basic thermodynamics, and machine drawing.

Semester 2 Modules (Scheduled)
  • MEC 121: Strength of Materials I — Direct stress, strain, shear force, and bending moments.
  • MEC 122: Fluid Mechanics I — Hydrostatic pressure, buoyancy, and Bernoulli's theorem.
  • MEC 123: Thermodynamics I — Gas laws, heat engines, and non-flow energy equations.
  • MEC 124: Engineering Drawing II — Assembly drawings, fasteners, and sectional details.
