MTH 311: Advanced Engineering Mathematics

Module 1: Second-Order Differential Equations, Laplace Transforms, and Fourier Analysis

1.1 Second-Order Linear Homogeneous ODEs

Second-order ordinary differential equations (ODEs) model mechanical vibration systems, spring-mass-damper dynamics, and electrical resonance circuits.

$$a \frac{d^2 y}{d x^2} + b \frac{d y}{d x} + c y = 0$$
Auxiliary Equation & General Solutions

$$a m^2 + b m + c = 0$$

Depending on the roots ($m_1, m_2$), the complementary function ($y_c$) takes one of three forms:

  • Real & Distinct Roots ($m_1 \neq m_2$): $y_c = C_1 e^{m_1 x} + C_2 e^{m_2 x}$
  • Real & Equal Roots ($m_1 = m_2 = m$): $y_c = (C_1 + C_2 x) e^{m x}$
  • Complex Conjugate Roots ($m = \alpha \pm i\beta$): $y_c = e^{\alpha x} (C_1 \cos \beta x + C_2 \sin \beta x)$

1.2 The Laplace Transform

The Laplace transform maps time-domain functions $f(t)$ into complex frequency-domain functions $F(s)$, turning differential equations into easily solved algebraic expressions.

Definition of Laplace Transform

$$\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\} = F(s) = \int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-st} f(t) \, dt$$

Standard Transform Pairs

$$\mathcal{L}\{1\} = \frac{1}{s}, \quad \mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\} = \frac{1}{s - a}, \quad \mathcal{L}\{\sin(\omega t)\} = \frac{\omega}{s^2 + \omega^2}$$
