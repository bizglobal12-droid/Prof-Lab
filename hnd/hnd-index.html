Higher National Diploma (HND) Curriculum

Advanced engineering analysis, thermal systems, fluid dynamics, and machine design

HND Year 1 — Semester 1

Advanced mechanics of materials, engineering thermodynamics, fluid power machinery, and engineering mathematics.

Semester 1 Modules
  • MEC 311: Advanced Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer

    Vapor power cycles, gas turbine cycles, conduction, convection, and thermal radiation.

  • MEC 312: Fluid Mechanics II & Turbo-Machinery

    Navier-Stokes fundamentals, boundary layer theory, centrifugal pumps, and impulse/reaction turbines.

  • MEC 313: Strength of Materials II

    Thick cylinders, asymmetrical bending, unsymmetrical stress analysis, and strain energy methods.

  • MTH 311: Advanced Engineering Mathematics

    Differential equations, Laplace transformations, Fourier series, and vector calculus.

HND Year 1 — Semester 2

Kinematics of machines, manufacturing technology, control engineering, and structural design.

Semester 2 Modules
  • MEC 321: Machine Design & Kinematics — Gear trains, governors, dynamic balancing, and shaft sizing.
  • MEC 322: Control Engineering — Open/closed loop feedback systems, transfer functions, and stability analysis.
  • MEC 323: Advanced Manufacturing Technology — CNC machining, non-traditional machining, and Metrology.
